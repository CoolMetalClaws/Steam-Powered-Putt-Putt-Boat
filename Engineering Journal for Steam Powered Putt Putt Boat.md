# Engineering Journal for Steam Powered Putt Putt Boat

## Problem

I have always been facinated with the idea of a small portable boat that is powered by only a candle, but the commercially sold versions are expensive where i am and are often imported, taking lots of time for delivery. This made me decide i wanted to create my own version, and challenged myself to make it use the least amount of fillament and materials, utilising mostly items found in every household. This design acheives just that, and is pretty easy to make as well, allowing anyone with a 3D printer and time to spare to make it.

## Step 1: Fabricate V1-3 (1 hrs 10 mins)

Ibegan designing with a genral boat shape in mind, without keeping in mind the dimensions and sizing for the boat. This lead to boats larger than required, wasting unecessary space and creating an overall heavier design, which sometimes tended to capsize. Versions 1-3 are all models that helped me shape the final design of the model, and provided the base shape for the final version. The first 3 designs also had a weighty look, and weren't the sleek, racing-boat style that i was aiming for.

## Step 2: Adapt the design for better propulsion (2 hrs 19 mins)

The first 4 designs also did not have a propulsion channel that could output more power, and the boat was unable to move due to this. The channels also had extremely slim walls that fractured under the slightest pressure and allowed leaks to grow larger unchecked. This resulted in a loss of power in the boat, as a vaccum was not formed due to a loss of pressure, which was required to suck water back into the boiler to repeat the propulsion cycle. 

## Step 3: Designing the final version (3 hrs 20 mins)

The 5th varaint of the design was flawless, with a minor problem; the boiler was unable to detach. This caused problems during assembly, making it extremely hard to access the space beneath the boiler to check for leaks and for applying epoxy. This provided me with the idea to make the boiler and fuel source detachable. This allowed for easy assembly and servicing if something should go wrong, and made the toy easier to use and re-fuel.

## Step 4: Complete project and Write README + JOURNAL (3 hrs 28 mins)

After finalising the project, i wrote a proper, detailed README and JOURNAL to upload my completed project. The README allows anyone to recreate my putt putt boat design with easy to understand instructions and details.
